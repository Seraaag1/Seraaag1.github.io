About me

Data work with a product mindset

I'm a Data Analyst and BI Developer based in Assiut, Egypt. I handle the whole chain: extracting and cleaning raw data with Python and SQL, modelling it, and turning it into interactive Power BI dashboards that decision-makers can read at a glance.

I spent a summer inside a retail sales team, so I know what the numbers mean on the shop floor — and I build dashboards around the questions the team really asks: where are we behind, who needs support, what should we focus on next.

I care about the details most dashboards skip: consistent design, custom navigation, clear colour logic and fast, honest KPIs.
